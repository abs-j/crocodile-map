🐊 Global Crocodile Species – Diversity, Size, and Conservation

Crocodiles are among the most iconic apex predators, yet many species are threatened by habitat loss, hunting, and climate change. This comprehensive dashboard explores the global distribution, physical characteristics, and conservation status of crocodilian species worldwide.

This analysis combines two complementary datasets: field observations from around the world showing real crocodile encounters, and comprehensive species information including maximum sizes, conservation status, and geographic ranges. Together, they provide both the "what we see" (observations) and the "what we know" (species characteristics) of crocodilian biodiversity.
